HOW IT ADDRESSES THE PROBLEM

  • Connects multiple agencies (NDRF, Police, Fire, EMS, Volunteers) on a single synchronized tactical command map.
  • Enables faster SOS response and precise victim tracking with under 30-second alert latency.

  • Optimizes resource allocation for rescue boats, ambulances, fire tenders, and relief supply stockpiles.
  • Empowers faster decision-making through automated multi-agency classification and AI triage.
